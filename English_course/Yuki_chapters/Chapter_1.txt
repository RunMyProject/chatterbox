Chapter 1 B: The "Morning Walk & Bus Ride".
After breakfast, She stepped outside for her morning exercise.
The sky was gray and misty, and the wind moved the branches of the trees.
Leaves fell gently on the wet pavement.
The park was full of life: pigeons cooed and walked on the grass, sparrows hopped between the shrubs, a robin sang on the fence, a swallow prepared its nest on a tree branch, and squirrels nibbled on nuts.
Tiny snails slid along the wet leaves, ladybugs climbed on stems, small cockroaches ran quickly, flies buzzed, and ants marched in a line.
Yuki smiled. “Everything is alive,” She whispered.
The trees were beautiful: oaks, maples, poplars, and lindens.
Their leaves were colored in shades of red, orange, yellow, and brown.
Some leaves shimmered with dew, sparkling in the soft morning light.
She noticed the hedges along the path, perfectly trimmed, with small flowers still visible.
She started her run.
Her heart beat faster, and She felt the fresh autumn air fill her lungs.
She did some push-ups, pull-ups, and jumping exercises near a park bench.
She tried a few parkour moves over a low wall.
Birds flew above her, and the smell of wet grass mixed with the faint scent of earth.
Looking at her smartwatch, She asked: “AI, what’s the temperature today?”
“Fifteen degrees Celsius, cloudy, with a chance of light rain,” the AI replied.
“Okay, I’ll keep my jacket on. Also, AI, where is Jupiter right now?”
“It’s in the eastern sky. You can see it after sunset,” said the AI.
Yuki smiled, imagining the distant planet orbiting slowly in space.
After thirty-five minutes, Yuki returned home, sweaty and energized.
She took a quick shower, washed her hair, and dried herself.
She picked her office clothes: white blouse, dark jeans, a scarf, gloves, warm coat, hat, and her favorite shoes.
She put on necklace, bracelet, and watch, applied lipstick and powder, and painted her nails soft pink.
She packed her bag: notebook, pens, eraser, tissues, some chocolates, and vitamins.
Before leaving, she looked at a photo of her ex-boyfriend in a small frame on her desk. 
They were at the beach last summer: sun shining on her skin, fish swimming near her feet, seashells, starfish, and small pebbles.
“I don’t want to think about him,” She whispered. “I need peace. Focus on today.”
She checked the weather again: heavy clouds, wind moving fallen leaves. “Maybe it will rain or snow later,” She thought. She opened the window briefly, feeling the cool autumn air.
She put on her coat, gloves, hat, scarf, grabbed her bag, and locked the door.
Her first stop was the bus to the underground station.
The street smelled of wet asphalt and autumn leaves.
She saw an old man walking his dog, a young mother carrying a baby, and teenagers riding bicycles.
Waiting for the bus, She heard someone humming “Walk Like an Egyptian” by The Bangles!.
Yuki smiled, recalling memories of the 80s.
Everyone was busy, everyone alive.
She smiled at a child holding a small backpack.
“Good morning,” She said quietly.
The bus arrived.
She climbed aboard, found a window seat, and watched people of many backgrounds: students speaking Spanish, a woman reading a newspaper in Mandarin, an African man humming the same song.
Princess Diana memories came to mind.
Finally, She arrived at the underground station.
“AI, show me the fastest route to the office,” She said.
“Go straight ahead, turn right after the bridge, the office is below the traffic lights.”
Yuki followed the instructions, observing the shiny tiles and busy people.
She felt ready, focused, and curious about the day ahead.
